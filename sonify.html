sonify

the vat's readings as sound, generated in your browser from the latest data the pi has published. each of the 8 electrodes is a voice in a slowly-evolving chord, placed in 3D space to mirror its position in the vat — use headphones to hear the spatial layout (surface above, bottom below, N/S front/back, E/W left/right).

this remote version reads the pi's published readings (updated roughly every 30 minutes when the pi is online). for the full-resolution live version (updates every minute), open /sonify on the lab network.

// how it maps

8 electrodes
each is a voice in a harmonic-series chord — bottom electrodes are the low partials, surface the high ones. each voice is positioned in 3D by its place in the vat. a voice gets louder and its pitch wavers as that electrode's voltage rises.
temperature
sets the root pitch of the whole chord. warmer = higher.
pH
brightness (a filter opening up). more alkaline = brighter.
ORP
spaciousness (reverb depth). more reducing (more negative) = more cavernous.
DO
a layer of air / breath noise. more dissolved oxygen = more audible breath.
