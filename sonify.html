// how it maps

8 electrodes
one voice each, louder as voltage rises. muting transposes the chord (centroid → cents) and switches the scale: bottom-heavy = Hirajōshi, top-heavy = Yo.

reading + intensity

filter's target value (saturation amount, cutoff, reverb wet, bell rate) + how much of that filter blends into the chord.

temperature
saturation drive.
pH
lowpass cutoff + tremolo rate.
ORP
reverb wet + sub sine at the root.
DO
panned bells (rate scales with the reading).
